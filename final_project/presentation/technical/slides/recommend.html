13 · Recommendation for National Treasury

A triage list, not an early warning

Do

  • Prioritise the 106 municipalities flagged for 2025 (102 at 80% or more)
  • Review each province's list before allocating support
  • Use the profiles to choose the kind of support

Do not

  • Allocate funding or interventions automatically
  • Judge or penalise municipalities or officials
  • Rely on it to warn that a clean municipality will fail
  • Use it after 2025 without retraining

Monitor yearly

  • Compare the forecast with the published 2025 outcomes
  • Retrain if macro-F1 falls below 0.866 or recall below 0.80
  • Check data quality on every new extract

Next version: add quarterly in-year section 71 data, audit findings and senior vacancies, and predict a change in status directly.

IDA117V · Predicting municipal audit outcomes · 14

Presenter: [project lead]. Our recommendation is realistic about what the model can do. It gives Treasury a consistent, explainable and documented priority list of municipalities already in difficulty, and the dashboard puts each one's history and finances in one place. It is not an early-warning system: it cannot see a clean municipality about to fail, and the within-group ranking did not reliably find those cases either. Monitoring thresholds come from the lower ends of the bootstrap confidence intervals. To become an early-warning tool, the next version needs in-year data and should model a change in status directly.
