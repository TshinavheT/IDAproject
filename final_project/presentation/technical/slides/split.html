Rules we kept

  • Imputation, scaling and encoding learned on training years only
  • Year, identifiers and raw opinion columns excluded
  • Tuning scored by walk-forward CV inside 2018–2021

A correction we made

Our first run used a random split and the year as a feature, letting the future inform the past. We re-ran every model on the time split; the champion changed from Random Forest to Logistic Regression.

IDA117V · Predicting municipal audit outcomes · 5 · Presenter: T Chaba

Presenter: T Chaba. Because we predict the future, we split by year. The model learns from 2018 to 2021, we pick the champion on 2022, and 2023 is the held-out test year, loaded exactly once at the very end. 2024 figures give a genuine forecast of the 2025 audit. Be open about the correction: the first run used a random split, which leaks future information; re-running on the time split is why the champion changed.
