IDA117V · Predicting municipal audit outcomes · 7 · Presenter: P Khosa

Presenter: P Khosa. This is the most important finding and we report it openly. A municipality's audit outcome this year is the strongest predictor of next year's. Our champion's yes/no decisions are identical to the simple rule "repeat last year's outcome". The financial ratios change the ranking within each group, not the decisions. Every later result, including the ablation and McNemar's test, confirms this.
