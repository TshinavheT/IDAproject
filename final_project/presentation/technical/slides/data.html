Source and licence

Municipal Money (municipaldata.treasury.gov.za): income and expenditure, financial position, debtors, repairs, irregular expenditure, grants and Auditor-General opinions. Free commercial and non-commercial reuse.

Privacy and quality

No personal data (POPIA): officials' names and contacts excluded. Self-reported figures include implausible values; early missing years are stored as zero; the grants extract is incomplete.

IDA117V · Predicting municipal audit outcomes · 4

Presenter: [data lead]. All data comes from National Treasury's Municipal Money platform, which allows free reuse, so the extracts are in our repository. We aggregated the line items into nine ratios per municipality-year. No personal data is used. We found and documented three data-quality problems: some implausible reported values, debtor and repairs data missing before 2020 and 2019 that is stored as zero, and a grants extract that appears cut off at 100,000 rows, so grant dependency is unreliable.
