IDA117V · Predicting municipal audit outcomes · 6 · Presenter: P Khosa

Presenter: P Khosa. Two traditional models and two neural networks, all inside the same preprocessing pipeline and trained on identical data. The neural networks use dropout, class-weighted loss and early stopping on a slice of the training years. We added a second baseline that simply repeats last year's outcome; keep it in mind for the next slide. Logistic Regression was chosen on the validation year: best macro-F1, best PR-AUC and the most stable cross-validation. The neural networks collapsed to about 0.53 in the fold trained on only two years.
