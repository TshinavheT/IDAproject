A missed municipality

Support arrives a year late, after services have already failed.

A false alarm

Scarce support and staff go where they were not needed.

IDA117V · Predicting municipal audit outcomes · 3

Presenter: [project lead]. The unit of prediction is a municipality in one financial year. We use that year's audited figures and its audit outcome to predict the outcome of the following year's audit, one full audit cycle ahead. The service-delivery success indicator is the share of failing municipalities Treasury reaches before their next audit. Macro-F1 is our primary metric because a missed municipality and a wasted intervention both have real costs.
