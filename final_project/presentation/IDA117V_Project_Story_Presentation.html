How it works

From public data to a decision in five steps

1 · Collect

Audited figures and Auditor-General opinions from Treasury’s Municipal Money API, 2018–2024

2 · Engineer

Nine financial-health ratios plus last year’s audit outcome for each municipality and year

3 · Learn

Six models compared on past years; the simplest reliable one, Logistic Regression, is locked in

4 · Forecast

The locked model scores every municipality’s next audit, with the reasons behind each score

5 · Act

A dashboard where officials drill from the national picture to one municipality

The whole pipeline reruns with one command, python run_all.py, and gives identical results every time.

Being honest about the limits

It knows which municipalities are struggling, not which are about to change

0 of 22

municipalities whose audit status changed were predicted correctly. Audit outcomes mostly repeat, and the model learned exactly that.

28.6%

false alarms in the Northern Cape, against 5–10% in other provinces: lists need a province-by-province review.

1,271%

irregular expenditure reported by one municipality: self-reported figures contain errors, and the worst performers submit the least data.

So we recommend it as a triage list for municipalities already in difficulty, never as an automatic funding or penalty tool.
