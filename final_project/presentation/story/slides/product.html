Presenter: [deployment lead]. Three views, from broad to specific. The overview gives the national picture and every number can be opened. The explorer filters municipalities by province, type, last audit opinion and risk band. The profile shows one municipality’s 2025 forecast, the reasons behind it, its history and how it compares with its peers and Treasury’s norms.
