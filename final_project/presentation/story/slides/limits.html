Seeing municipal audit failure coming · 11 · Presenter: T Mabena

Presenter: T Mabena. We want to be clear about what the product cannot do. Because audit outcomes repeat from year to year, the model is excellent at confirming which municipalities are in trouble, but it got none of the 22 municipalities that changed status in the test year right. Its errors are also uneven across provinces. And the data is self-reported. That is why our recommendation is a triage list that supports human judgement, not an early-warning or decision system.
