Why we chose this project

Four reasons this problem was worth solving

1

A real service-delivery problem

Audit outcomes show whether a municipality can manage the money behind basic services.

2

A clear decision-maker

National Treasury and the provincial treasuries decide each year where scarce support goes.

3

Authoritative, open data

Treasury’s Municipal Money covers every municipality, is free to reuse and holds no personal data.

4

A measurable outcome

The Auditor-General publishes an opinion every year, so every prediction can be checked against reality.

Seeing municipal audit failure coming · 3 · Presenter: T Mabena

Presenter: T Mabena. The brief asked for a real South African service-delivery problem with a stakeholder who makes a decision. Audit outcomes fit every requirement: they matter for services, Treasury has a concrete yearly decision to make, the data is official and openly licensed with no personal information, and the outcome is published every year, so we can test our predictions honestly.
