Seeing municipal audit failure coming · 8 · Presenter: R Tabohane

Presenter: R Tabohane. We kept 2023 aside for the whole project and used it once, at the end. The model classified 90.8 percent of municipalities correctly, flagged 90 of the 103 that went on to fail, and raised 9 false alarms among 137 healthy ones. The 95 percent bootstrap interval for macro-F1 is 0.87 to 0.94, so the result is not a fluke of one sample. The technical deck and the report have the full evidence.
