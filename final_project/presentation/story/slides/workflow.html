Presenter: [deployment lead]. Walk through a year in the life of the tool. The official starts with the national list, narrows it to their province, opens each municipality to understand why it is flagged, and then decides what support to give. The model never makes the decision. When the Auditor-General publishes the next outcomes, the forecast is checked and the model is retrained on the newest year.
