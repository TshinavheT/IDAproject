Seeing municipal audit failure coming · 2

Presenter: [project lead]. Walk the chain from left to right. Problems in a municipality’s finances show up first as weak cash and poor collection, then as unpaid suppliers and neglected infrastructure, and finally as service failures that residents feel. The Auditor-General’s opinion is the official verdict, but it is published long after the financial year ends. In our data, 45.5 percent of municipality-years were followed by a poor audit, so this is not a rare event.
