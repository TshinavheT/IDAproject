How it works

From public data to a decision in five steps

1 · Collect

Audited figures and Auditor-General opinions from Treasury’s Municipal Money API, 2018–2024

2 · Engineer

Nine financial-health ratios plus last year’s audit outcome for each municipality and year

3 · Learn

Six models compared on past years; the simplest reliable one, Logistic Regression, is locked in

4 · Forecast

The locked model scores every municipality’s next audit, with the reasons behind each score

5 · Act

A dashboard where officials drill from the national picture to one municipality

The whole pipeline reruns with one command, python run_all.py, and gives identical results every time.

Seeing municipal audit failure coming · 6 · Presenter: T Chaba, then P Khosa

Presenter: T Chaba, then P Khosa. Step 1: we download audited financial statements and audit opinions for every municipality from Treasury’s API. Step 2: we turn hundreds of line items into nine health ratios, such as months of cash on hand and the share of bills collected, plus last year’s audit outcome. Step 3: we train and compare six models on past years and lock in the best. Step 4: the locked model scores each municipality’s next audit and explains why. Step 5: the dashboard puts it in officials’ hands. Everything is reproducible with one command.
